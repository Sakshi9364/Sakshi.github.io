01 — Overview

What this course covers

Marketing Management (26MKM501) takes the classical marketing mix and rebuilds it for a digital-first buyer — covering the marketing environment, consumer behaviour, product & pricing decisions, distribution, and promotion, with a case study embedded in every unit.

Unit 1

Marketing Concept & the Digital Ecosystem

Nature & scope, 4Ps → 4Cs, CRM/E-CRM, the search-social-mobile environment.

Case: Digital CRM at education discovery platforms

Unit 2

Consumer Behaviour & the Connected Buyer

Buyer behaviour theory, decision process, segmentation, digital footprints.

Case: Decoding the Gamer

Unit 3

Product & Price Decisions

Product mix, branding, NPD & PLC, pricing strategy, freemium models.

Case: The Digital Agency Model

Unit 4

Distribution & Physical Decisions

Channel design, D2C & e-commerce, retailing, omnichannel logistics.

Case: Event Marketing Distribution (TEDx)

Unit 5

Promotion, Legal & Ethical Aspects

Promotion mix, SEO & social, consumer protection, data privacy.

Case: Hyper-Local SEO & Promotion

Course outcomes

By the end of the course you will be able to —

  1. CO1 · UnderstandDescribe various aspects of digital & social media marketing practices.
  2. CO2 · AnalyzeRecognize and differentiate between traditional & social media platforms.
  3. CO3 · ApplyAcquire the skill to engage consumers online.
  4. CO4 · CreateBuild organizational competencies by way of digital marketing practices and cost considerations.
  5. CO5 · ApplyDemonstrate an understanding of the latest digital practices for marketing & promotion.
